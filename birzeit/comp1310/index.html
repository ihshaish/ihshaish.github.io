About the Course

What it covers

How a computer is built and how it represents data, numbering systems, algorithms and pseudocode, and an introduction to programming in C, alongside the ethics of computing. Weekly labs cover MS Office, algorithms and C.

Assessment

Midterm exam30%
Lab work (5 quizzes + mini project)30%
Final exam40%

Study Companions

Computer Basics

A short walk through Lecture 1: what happens between a key press and a result on the screen, what the CPU and the different kinds of memory each do, how a computer starts up, and what the operating system is for. Ends with self-check questions.

Open companion

Numbering Systems

Both numbering lectures in one place, built up from the question of why computers count in binary. Covers converting between bases (and why the methods work), fractions, binary addition and subtraction, negative numbers in two's complement, and how integers, characters and real numbers are stored. Includes a bit board, step-by-step converters and practice questions with answers.
